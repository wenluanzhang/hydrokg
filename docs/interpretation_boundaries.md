# Interpretation boundaries

HydroKG complements the frozen curated table by representing recurring
scientific concepts and record provenance as explicit relationships. The graph
makes cross-record comparison and contextual retrieval easier; it does not
replace the underlying records.

Graph links encode curated, normalized, declared, or deterministic
rule-derived relationships. Connectivity does not establish causality, rank
materials, or experimentally validate a physical mechanism.

`mechanism_simple` and related mechanism descriptions are expert-harmonized
interpretive labels retained from the curated dataset. They should be read as
reported or curated context, not as independent mechanism assignments made by
the graph.

Rule-derived resistance, power, pathway, warning, design-variable, and
reporting contexts organize records and support reproducible queries. A rule
match means that a record satisfies configured conditions; it is not causal or
mechanistic confirmation.

Missing reporting means the relevant information was unavailable in the
curated source record. It is not negative evidence and does not experimentally
exclude a property, condition, or contribution.

The low-resistance, NaCl/Na+ context, and four-criterion results describe the
frozen 159-record corpus. They should not be generalized beyond that curated
scope without additional data and validation.
